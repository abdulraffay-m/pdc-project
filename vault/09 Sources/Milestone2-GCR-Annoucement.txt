As per the semester project schedule, Milestone 2—Project Proposal Submission is due on Monday, 23 November 2026.

Each group is required to prepare and submit a project proposal based on its selected research paper. Your proposal must clearly demonstrate your understanding of the paper, the proposed implementation scope, its technical complexity, and your planned contribution.

Your proposal must cover the following:

1. Paper Understanding: Explain the graph problem, research objective, core contribution, and algorithm/methodology of the selected paper.
2. Selected Scope and Complexity: Clearly define what you intend to implement, what you will exclude, and justify the technical difficulty and computational challenges.
3. Proposed Contribution: Explain how you plan to extend or improve the selected approach using parallel computing concepts covered in this course (OpenMP, MPI, SIMD, or OpenCL).
4. Implementation Plan: Describe your baseline implementation, proposed parallelization strategy, and expected improvements.
5. Evaluation Plan: Explain how you will validate correctness and evaluate performance.
6. References and Attribution: Properly acknowledge the selected research paper, existing code repositories, datasets, and other resources you intend to use.

Evaluation – 30 Marks

Milestone 2 will be evaluated according to the following criteria:

- Understanding of the selected research paper: 10 Marks
- Scope, challenge, and complexity justification: 10 Marks
- Proposal, implementation plan, and attribution: 5 Marks
- Presentation and defense (within 5 minutes): 5 Marks

Important Instructions:
- Every group must be prepared to explain and defend its proposal.
- Your selected scope must be technically meaningful and achievable within the project timeline.
- Simply reproducing or cloning an existing repository will not be considered an original contribution.
- Marks will be awarded based on technical depth, complexity, reasoning, and the quality of your proposed contribution, not merely on the completion of the task.
