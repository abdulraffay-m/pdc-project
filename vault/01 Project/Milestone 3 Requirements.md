## Submission rules
- **Acknowledge** all external code, repositories, libraries, datasets and AI tools. Track them as we go in [[Acknowledgements]].
- **Viva in dead week**: every member must understand the implementation. **Individual contributions and understanding are graded.**

## What this means for us
- Keep comments in submitted code, even though some of us prefer none. It's a grading requirement.
- Each person must be able to defend their own part without help. That's why [[Roles]] gives everyone one clear story.
- The report comes last, but write down numbers and observations in [[Results Log]] while running, so the report is mostly assembly.
